# Profile evidence policy

The profile is a map to source repositories, not a substitute for their evidence.

Every featured quantitative claim should be traceable to the linked repository and, where practical, a named evaluation artifact or methodology. The machine-readable profile manifest is checked in CI to ensure featured projects keep their repository and evidence links.

Do not publish production performance, uptime, accuracy, scale, or cost numbers on the profile unless the underlying repository records the dataset/workload, method, environment, denominator, tool versions, and producing commit.

Capabilities and architecture descriptions may be qualitative. Design targets must be labeled as targets rather than measured results.
